# 📊 Sales Prediction & Customer Segmentation

**BACS3013 — Data Science Assignment**

A data science project focused on analyzing business data through **sales prediction and customer segmentation**. The project applies data analysis and machine learning techniques to uncover business insights, predict future sales, and identify different customer groups based on their characteristics and purchasing behavior.

By combining predictive analytics and customer segmentation, this project aims to support data-driven business decisions and improve understanding of customer behavior.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze historical sales and customer data.
- Identify patterns and trends in sales performance.
- Develop predictive models to estimate sales.
- Segment customers based on their characteristics and purchasing behavior.
- Generate meaningful insights to support business decision-making.

## 📈 Project Components

### 1. Sales Prediction

Develop predictive models to estimate sales based on historical data and relevant features.

**Main focus:**
- Explore historical sales data.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Identify important variables influencing sales.
- Train and evaluate predictive models.
- Analyze prediction results and model performance.

**Objective:** Understand sales patterns and use historical information to predict future sales.

### 2. Customer Segmentation

Apply customer segmentation techniques to group customers according to similarities in their characteristics and purchasing behavior.

**Main focus:**
- Analyze customer characteristics and purchasing patterns.
- Perform data preprocessing and feature selection.
- Apply clustering techniques to identify customer groups.
- Visualize and interpret customer segments.
- Identify meaningful patterns within different customer groups.

**Objective:** Understand customer behavior and identify different customer segments to support business strategies.

---

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection** – Obtain the datasets required for sales prediction and customer segmentation.
2. **Data Preprocessing** – Clean, transform, and prepare the datasets.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – Explore patterns, relationships, and trends in the data.
4. **Feature Engineering** – Select and transform relevant features.
5. **Model Development** – Build sales prediction and customer segmentation models.
6. **Model Evaluation** – Evaluate model performance and analyze the results.
7. **Data Visualization** – Present findings and business insights.
